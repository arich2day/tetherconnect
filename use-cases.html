Who it's for

Teams who can't afford
to guess what left.

These are the situations Tether is built for. They're target scenarios by vertical — not named customers; we don't claim customers we don't have. If one of them is your world, a two-week pilot shows you exactly what Tether would catch on your real traffic.

Fintech · PCI

Secrets and keys headed into AI tools.

Customer IDs and cloud access keys ending up in a Cursor or Claude Code prompt. What these teams need: a deterministic floor that always blocks named secrets before they leave, and an audit trail a PCI assessor can actually check.

Healthtech · HIPAA

Patient data pasted in for context.

A record paraphrased into a prompt to get better help from an AI tool. What these teams need: a pause for a human on the genuinely ambiguous moment — an operator decides in seconds — plus a signed receipt showing what was decided and by which policy.

AI-native startup · SOC 2

The auditor asks what left through AI tools.

A fast-moving team whose reviewer wants evidence, not assurances. What these teams need: proof each decision is real and tied to the policy that made it — verifiable offline, without trusting the vendor.
