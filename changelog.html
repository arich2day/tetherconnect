What's shipped.

Recent capabilities, most recent first. This list reflects shipped functionality; roadmap items are called out as such elsewhere on the site.

Recent

SIEM adapters & offline verifier

  • SIEM adapters shipped: Splunk HEC, Microsoft Sentinel, and syslog (RFC 5424).
  • Offline attestation verifier — tools/verify-attestation.js, zero dependencies, re-checks any decision without trusting us.

Identity & device posture

  • SCIM provisioning via Jackson, with role-from-groups mapping and full audit of adds and removals.
  • Device-posture export to Okta Device Trust, Microsoft Graph managed-device, and CSV.

Enforcement & policy core

  • Deterministic pattern floor, just-in-time approval queue, and an append-only audit log.
  • Signed policy distribution — Ed25519, roughly ten seconds to reach the fleet.
