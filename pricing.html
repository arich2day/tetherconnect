FAQ

What the price does
and does not include.

Is judge inference included?

Judge inference is customer-supplied. A cloud judge bills from your own model vendor — Tether bills nothing for judge calls. A local judge runs on the developer's machine or a server you host, at zero per-call cost. You tune how often the judge samples, so the cost stays predictable.

Why is the judge cost not on the bill?

Model costs vary by 100x across vendors. Folding them into a per-seat number would distort the economics for half our customers. At the default sample rate, judge inference is negligible relative to the model spend your developers already generate — and $0 on a local judge. We'll give you a concrete range for your seat count and judge model on request.

Are custom integrations included?

First three SAML / SCIM connector configurations are included. Subsequent custom integrations bill at $2,500 / connector flat. Custom data-residency requirements (EU-only Overwatch, on-prem-only) bill at +30% to base list.

What's the cloud infrastructure cost?

You run one small per-tenant service (Cloud Run on GCP or ECS Fargate on AWS) and pay your cloud vendor directly — plan for roughly a few tens of dollars a month, depending on cloud and traffic. It's predictable because the service bills by instance-hour, not per request, so the bill is flat per tenant. We'll size it for your environment on request.
