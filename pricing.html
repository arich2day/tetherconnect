FAQ

What the price does
and does not include.

Is judge inference included?

Judge inference is customer-supplied. Cloud judges bill from your model vendor — Tether bills nothing for judge calls. Local Ollama judges run on the developer's machine or a customer-hosted server at zero per-call cost. Operators tune the sample rate (default 3%, hard-capped at 10%) so cost stays predictable.

Why is the judge cost not on the bill?

Model costs vary by 100x across vendors. Folding them into a per-seat number would distort the economics for half our customers. Concrete range so you can sanity-check: at the default 3% sample rate, a 50-seat fleet runs ~$0.20–$8.80/month on gpt-4o-mini (cloud judge); ~$0 on local Ollama. Source: 2026-06 OpEx review (docs/reviews/03-business.md §1.1).

Are custom integrations included?

First three SAML / SCIM connector configurations are included. Subsequent custom integrations bill at $2,500 / connector flat. Custom data-residency requirements (EU-only Overwatch, on-prem-only) bill at +30% to base list.

What's the cloud infrastructure cost?

Per-tenant Cloud Run or ECS Fargate runs roughly $15-65 / month on top of the per-seat license, depending on cloud and traffic. Predictable: Cloud Run bills by instance-hour, not per request, so the bill is flat per tenant. You pay your cloud vendor directly.
